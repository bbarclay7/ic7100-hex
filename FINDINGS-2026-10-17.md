# 2026-10-17: browser TX audio had a 48 Hz gap in every frame

**Symptom (nets, desktop and phone):** "multiple paths to the microphone" (VHF), "talking in a hallway, distorted" (HF),
"intermittent, with an AC hum" (first phone check-in). With and without CESSB, any mic (AirPods or MacBook).

**Cause:** `webrtc.py` forwarded `bytes(rf.planes[0])` after PyAV resampled each 20 ms frame. The plane buffer is padded for
alignment (960 mono s16 samples arrive as 1984 bytes), so every 960 real samples were followed by 32 zero samples:
a 0.67 ms hole every 20.67 ms, about 48 times a second, gated by the voice.

**How it was found:** a debug tap (`IC7100_TX_DUMP=dir`, in `AlsaPlayback`) saved exactly what the radio is sent. The audio showed
jumps on a 992-sample grid (concentration 1.00; other grids about 0.1) and exact zeros at samples 960-991 of every block.
The same trill was audible in the FT-710 recordings, in a control played straight into the codec, and in the tap, which is what
ruled out the radio, the receiving rig and the mic. Idle recordings before keying had no impulses.

**Fix:** send `bytes(rf.planes[0])[:rf.samples * 2]`. New test `test_forwarded_audio_has_no_alignment_padding` fails without it.
After the fix the tap has 0 jump events in 12.8 s (201 before in 14.4 s) and no zero runs; listened to: "continuous and good".

**Not affected:** the CESSB RF measurements (FINDINGS-2026-10-08.md, `sdr-tests/cessb-rf/METHOD.md`); they played WAVs into the codec from
hex and never used the browser path.

Tools: `sdr-tests/cessb-rf/mic_analyze.py` (band split, spectrogram). Raw tap files and listening copies stay out of git.

## Monitor dial was off (same day)
The FT-710 monitor was found tuned to 3757.151 MHz LSB instead of 3758.000 (cause and time unknown, but definitely during the day's series).
All FT-710 recordings from that session (`mic_A_airpods_cessboff`, `mic_B_macbook_cessboff`) have their audio shifted down about 849 Hz, so their
spectral readings ("energy at 300-700 Hz", "boxy") are invalid. Everything after the tap was measured at the radio's input and is unaffected.
The 2026-10-08/09 RF series is not affected (frequency verified and the monitor was locked then).
